## Rules

- Two players share the same device — White starts at the bottom, Black at the top
- Players alternate turns. White goes first
- **Goal:** Get any one of your pawns to the opposite back row before your opponent does
- If a player has no valid moves, they lose
- Captured pawns are removed from the board permanently

## Movement

Pawns follow standard chess pawn movement rules:

- Move **forward one square** into an empty space
- Capture **diagonally forward** onto a square occupied by an opponent's pawn
- Pawns cannot move backwards

## Controls

| Action | How |
|---|---|
| Select a pawn | Click on one of your pawns |
| Move | Click a highlighted square |
| Deselect | Click anywhere else |
| Restart | Click the ↺ Restart button |

Valid moves are highlighted in blue. Capture squares are highlighted in red.
