# Knight Journey

Guide a white knight across the chessboard and find the shortest path to capture the black king. Every position is a tactical puzzle where precision matters — one wrong move can block your route or leave you trapped.

Place pieces freely to create your own challenges, from simple setups to complex defensive formations that demand careful planning and foresight. You’ll need to think like a chess player and a problem solver at the same time, finding clean paths through dangerous territory.

Each puzzle is about efficiency: reach the king in the fewest moves possible, or test yourself by designing positions that are intentionally difficult to solve.
