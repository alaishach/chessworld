# Bishop Maze

Navigate a chess bishop through a series of increasingly complex mazes. The catch? You can only move diagonally — just like a real bishop.

Each level presents a grid with walls blocking certain paths. Find a route from your starting square to the glowing star goal, and try to do it in par or fewer moves. Move hints show you where you can go, and your trail shows where you've been.

10 handcrafted levels take you from a simple open board to dense, winding mazes that will test your spatial thinking.
